## 🧰 Troubleshooting Common Issues

### FuelSwitch-AI Won't Open
Right-click the app and select "Open" to bypass the Gatekeeper warning. Alternatively, go to System Preferences > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway."

### Gauges Show 0%
Make sure your AI tools are installed and you've added at least one account. Also verify you've granted FuelSwitch-AI access to your configuration folders.

### Countdown Not Updating
Check your internet connection and make sure you're logged into your AI tool accounts. FuelSwitch-AI needs to read the current session data.
